Corrections, scenarios and teaching experience are welcome.

- **A correction to the paper or the model.** Open an issue with the correction form: what is wrong, where, and the source that shows it.
- **A scenario from your setting.** Use the scenario form; say which settings you changed and why. Leave out anything that identifies a patient, a learner or a colleague.
- **A change by pull request.** Run `bash ops/verify.sh` first. In the pull request, write your intent in your own words; it is kept as you wrote it.

Contributors are credited for their contributions; the originator's credit is never removed or replaced. By contributing you agree that your contribution is licensed as the file it changes is (text CC BY-SA 4.0, code GPL-3.0-or-later).
