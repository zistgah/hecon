Read this first, cold.

**What it is.** The paper "From Educational Harvesting to Productive Capacity" by Abhishek Choudhary (AyeAI), a conceptual health economics framework for AI-assisted clinical reasoning education, published with a scenario model, a site and six Colab notebooks for medicos. Version 1.0.0, 5 November 2026.

**How it is made.** The paper's five files live in docs/paper and are the published text. The model is hecon/ (Python, standard library only) with an identical browser twin, docs/hecon-model.js. Notebooks are generated by tools/build_notebooks.py; notebook 05 is the author's own workbench carried from origin/received. The site is docs/, served by GitHub Pages from main:/docs.

**State.** Built and executed: the model, its tests, the parity check, the site in a DOM harness, and all six notebooks headless. Written and tested against stand-ins only: the endpoint AI provider and refcheck's network calls. Not run: anything that reaches the internet or acts in the reader's name (model downloads, terminology lookups, timestamps, minting); those steps are off by default.

**Open.** Item by item in docs/paper/WORKING-NOTES.md: the review methodology, declarations of funding and interests, and journal-specific formatting are the author's to give. References still open are listed in REFERENCES.md with how they will be checked.

**Privacy, stated once.** Nothing here holds a real person's data. The teaching case is synthetic. The privacy steps follow the strictest of the major frameworks worldwide.

**Components.** COMPONENTS.md names what is reused, by repository and commit, and what is hosted here until it moves home.
